For async startup/cache/resource owners: capture generation plus exact operation/resource identity before the first await; after every await revalidate before registry/UI publication; shutdown fences the generation, removes visible authority, aborts and waits under an absolute deadline, and keeps a handled late-cleanup continuation. A forced-closing pending owner must immediately close resources registered after its drain deadline. Reverse-order tests must cover late publication, late resource registration, replacement startup, and non-cooperative close.
