Apply explicit count, byte, time, and replay bounds before full allocation. Streaming readers cancel on overflow and every early return; replay buffers preserve checkpoint/patch contiguity per stream or emit resync-required; process jobs remain active while any owned process group survives; oversized artifacts use one private bounded store with deterministic eviction and TTL. A bound is incomplete if measurement itself serializes the full value or cleanup releases ownership before termination is proven.
