Normal non-exhausted completed turns defer and reassess compaction. Only an exhausted request with no queued messages may recover through an unowned native compaction; identify that recovery using a leading instruction marker, not substring matching. Root and child extension surfaces may differ in ownership features but must share completed-turn cancellation and lifecycle error isolation.
