When a dispatch or monitor action can allocate timers, listeners, registry slots, child work, or delayed analysis, establish throwable controls before admission/work, clean every partial admission on setup failure, and after each await revalidate the exact binding/session owner before publication. Iterate mutable owner maps from a tick-start snapshot so remove+add replacements are not processed by the stale tick.
